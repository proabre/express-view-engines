EJS partials are reusable pieces of an EJS page, such as a navbar, header, footer, or sidebar.

They help avoid repeating the same HTML code on multiple pages.

Example folder structure:

views/
partials/
nav.ejs
footer.ejs
index.ejs
about.ejs

To include a partial:

<%- include("partials/nav") %>

Example:

index.ejs:
<%- include("partials/nav") %>

<h1>Home Page</h1>

<%- include("partials/footer") %>

Main idea:

EJS partials = reusable EJS components.

They make your Express application cleaner, easier to maintain, and reduce duplicate code.
